Privacy Policy (English)

JJY Simulator does not collect, store, share, or track any personal data.

  1. No Data Collected: The app does not collect names, email addresses, location data, device identifiers, diagnostics, or usage analytics.
  2. Fully Local & Offline: JJY time-code synthesis and the bundled world map run entirely on your device. No data is transmitted to external servers.
  3. Permissions: The app only outputs audio to your device’s audio route and does not request access to microphone, camera, location, or contacts.
  4. No Ads or Third-Party Tracking: The app contains no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.
